My PhD student George Tridimas has put his first preprint [*Infinite Loop Spaces and Group Actions on Strongly Self-Absorbing C\*-Algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20105) on the arXiv today. In this work we study the classifying spaces arising from crossed modules associated to unital C\*-algebras that first came up in [*G-kernels and Croosed Modules*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04134). We show that for strongly self-absorbing C\*-algebras these spaces have infinite loop space structures.
