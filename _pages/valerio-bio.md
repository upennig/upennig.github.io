Valerio worked on equivariant \(C^*\)-algebra bundles. His PhD was EPSRC-funded and gave an equivariant classification of \(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\)-equivariant UHF-algebra bundles. More about his work can be found on [his website](https://sites.google.com/view/valeriobianchi/home-page?authuser=0). His viva was in December 2025.
