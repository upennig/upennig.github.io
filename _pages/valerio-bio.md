Valerio works on \(C^*\)-algebras with applications in topological quantum field theories. His EPSRC-funded project, *K-theory of C\*-algebras with applications in TQFT*, gave an equivariant classification of UHF-algebra bundles with applications to the Freed–Hopkins–Teleman theorem. More about his work can be found on [his website](https://sites.google.com/view/valeriobianchi/home-page?authuser=0).
