## online tools for mathematicians

- [OASIS - Operator Algebras Searchable Information Site](https://operatoralgebras.org/)
- [Mathoverflow.net](http://mathoverflow.net)
- [arXiv](http://arxiv.org)
- [arxivist - your personal guide to the arxiv](http://arxivist.com)
- [scimeter](https://scimeter.org)
- [Google Scholar](http://scholar.google.com)
- [Mathscinet](http://www.ams.org/mathscinet) (requires University account)
- [Zentralblatt Math](https://www.zbmath.org)
- [GDZ (Göttingen Digitisation Centre)](http://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/en/gdz/)
- [The Manifold Atlas](http://www.map.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/index.php/Main_Page)
- [Detexify](http://detexify.kirelabs.org/classify.html) (draw a symbol and get the LaTeX code for it)
- [Mathpix](https://mathpix.com) (convert screenshots to LaTeX code)
- [Online Videos of the MSRI](https://legacy.slmath.org/videos/dashboard)
- [Seminars archive of the Isaac Newton Institute](https://www.newton.ac.uk/webseminars)
