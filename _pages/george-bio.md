Georgios (George) is working on topological invariants of G-kernels on strongly self-absorbing C\*-algebras. His Heilbronn Institute-funded project, *Symmetries of C\*-algebras through the lens of stable homotopy theory*, studies homotopy types of classifying spaces associated to topological 2-groups arising from G-kernels and cocycle actions on strongly self-absorbing C\*-algebras.
